I am writing to you about the current \role\ position in \location. I believe that with my qualifications, skill set and experience I make an ideal candidate for this vacancy.

I have a natural aptitude and passion for software engineering as proven throughout my time at Senacor Technologies AG and during my bachelor’s degree studies at the Edinburgh Napier University, where I have achieved a First Class in Software Engineering. I am confident in all aspects of the design, development and testing of computer software and I am keen to continue to develop my abilities. My technical skills include Java, Spring Boot, C++, C\#, JavaScript/TypesScript, VueJS, Python, Docker, AWS, SQL (DB2, Sybase, MySQL, Postgres) and MongoDB.

To date, I have gained almost two years of professional experience in software development, at high-calibre organisations including Morgan Stanley, MarketAxess and my current employer, Senacor Technologies AG. This has significantly enhanced my commercial acumen and expertise. For example, during my internship at Morgan Stanley, I drove the design, migration and enhancement of an attractive software feature which led to client retention as well as the attraction of new clients. Moreover during my latest Project, I was able to take part in the solution design process and took responsibility for most of our infrastructure components. In addition, I have consistently demonstrated that I have excellent communication skills and I can successfully manage team relationships at all levels, remaining open to feedback and providing a first-class service to all internal and external stakeholders.

During my internship at MarketAxess, I was able to gain an initial insight into the fixed income market and specifically fixed income electronic trading venues, which I am now keen to expand. I have been impressed with Bloomberg ever since I started taking an interest in Financial Services and after previously having the chance to interview for an internship at Bloomberg in London I am confident this is the ideal next step in my career.

Thank you for taking the time to consider my application. I look forward to hearing from you in due course.
